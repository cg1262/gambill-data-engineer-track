The Launch path

Phase by phase

Bronze → silver → gold → launch. Each phase ends in a portfolio artifact; the whole path ends in a defensible, governed data product.

Phase 1

Foundations

Weeks 1–13 · bronze
CLI/Git, Python, SQL, relational modeling — real-data labs from day one.
Phase 2

Core data engineering

Weeks 14–30 · silver
One running medallion pipeline → SCD2, MDM golden records, incremental, DQ.
Phase 3

Platform & production

Weeks 31–43 · gold
Unity Catalog, Lakeflow, streaming, DABs/CI-CD, PHI governance — productionized.
Phase 4

Advanced & launch

Weeks 44–50
Architecture, elective, interview prep, and your own independent capstone.

How it works

Self-paced, with accountability

A weekly live review with a "defend your decision" hot seat — where theory becomes something you can articulate under questioning.
